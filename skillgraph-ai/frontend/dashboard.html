How it works

  1. AI Text Extraction: PyPDF2 and python-docx parse the raw content from your document.
  2. NLP Skill Extraction: spaCy lemmatization and pattern dictionary identify tech stack components in your work history.
  3. Gap Analysis & Metrics: Your skills are compared to standard industry curriculum definitions for your target goal.
  4. Action Plan: A month-by-month learning schedule and certification tracks are generated to guide your study.
